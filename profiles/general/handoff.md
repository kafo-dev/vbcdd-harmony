# Handoff

Write down, for the conversation that follows, everything it needs to carry on.
Write it as the first message of that conversation, addressed to yourself: no
preamble, no heading, and no mention of this instruction.

- What the owner asked for, and what was settled, in their own terms.
- What you found out and what you changed, naming the files and the commands.
- What is still open, and what you would do next.
